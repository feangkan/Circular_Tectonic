# Assessment Framework — Official Rubric

**Source: RMIT Studio Learning + Teaching Document**

---

## Design Skills Criteria

**Expected to demonstrate:**
- Architectural proposals informed by circular design principles, integrating reclaimed materials, structural reasoning, computational design, and fabrication strategies within a **coherent tectonic system**
- An iterative design process combining material exploration, computational modelling, physical prototyping, and critical reflection
- Critical engagement with reclaimed materials as **active generators** of form/construction logic/spatial organisation (not secondary "sustainability add-ons")
- Translation of concepts into **buildable tectonic systems** via digital fabrication, structural articulation, assembly strategy
- Awareness of circular economy principles, material innovation, collaborative design-build practice, environmental responsibility

**Assessed on:**
- Clarity/coherence of circular design strategy (materials + structure + computation integration)
- Depth of iterative exploration, material experimentation, prototyping, computational investigation
- Synthesis of material intelligence + fabrication logic + tectonic thinking into a resolved proposal
- Degree of innovation, constructability, and meaningful engagement with circular principles

---

## Communication Skills Criteria

**Expected to:**
- Use drawings, diagrams, digital workflows, physical models to communicate architectural ideas, material systems, fabrication strategies, tectonic relationships
- Employ scaled prototypes + computational models to demonstrate structural organisation, material behaviour, assembly logic
- Integrate material studies, fabrication documentation, process diagrams into presentations showing evolution of thinking

**Assessed on:**
- Clarity/precision of drawings, models, diagrams
- Effectiveness of physical prototypes (fabrication logic, assembly sequence, spatial qualities)
- Integration of computational workflows + material research + fabrication process into overall narrative

## Grade Descriptors

| Grade | Range | Description |
|-------|-------|--------------|
| **High Distinction** | 80–100% | Exceptionally clear understanding; well organised, sustained presentation and response to critique; addresses all objectives to a high standard; **creative insight and originality** |
| **Distinction** | 70–79% | Strong grasp of subject matter/key issues; addresses all objectives, several to high standard; clearly developed presentation; evidence of creative, solid work |
| **Credit** | 60–69% | Competent understanding; addresses all objectives, some reasonably well; clearly developed, well prepared/presented |
| **Pass** | 50–59% | Satisfactory; addresses all objectives but generally lacking depth/breadth; basic comprehension; may be poorly prepared/presented |
| **Fail** | 0–49% | Lack of understanding; minimal/inadequate comprehension; doesn't address all objectives; inadequate depth/breadth, possibly incomplete |

## Core Learning Outcomes (Master of Architecture)

1. Apply design knowledge to an architectural problem
2. Critically analyse, evaluate, and make informed judgement on technical, programmatic, theoretical, historical, and professional issues and their implications for building design
3. Integrate practical and theoretical knowledge into the design process within time constraints
4. Investigate and comprehend architecture's relationship to location, program, form, and representation
5. Demonstrate/articulate design skills through an iterative, considered process (concept → design development)
6. Communicate design ideas to a high standard, through the building design and its representation

---

## What This Means for My Weekly Work

Every weekly deliverable I produce should be traceable to these criteria. Self-check questions before each submission:

- [ ] Does this show the reclaimed material **generating** the design (not decorating it)?
- [ ] Is there evidence of **iteration** (multiple versions, not just a final answer)?
- [ ] Does it connect **material → computation → fabrication → assembly** as one system?
- [ ] Is the communication (drawing/diagram/model) precise and legible on its own?
- [ ] Would a reviewer see **originality**, not just competence?
