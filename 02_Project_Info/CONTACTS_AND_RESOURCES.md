### Reclaimed Materials & Deconstruction
- USGBC Deconstruction Guidelines
- Salvo (UK salvage database): https://www.salvo.co.uk/
- Reclaimed Elements Australia

### 3D Scanning & Point Cloud Processing
- CloudCompare (open-source): http://www.cloudcompare.org/

- Grasshopper (Rhino plugin)
