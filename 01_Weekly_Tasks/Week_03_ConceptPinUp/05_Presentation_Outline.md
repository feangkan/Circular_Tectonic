## Section-by-Section Talking Points

**1. Framing (1 min)** — Open with the computational lead: a differential-growth algorithm generates a variable, non-standard structural/skin network. State the strategic insight up front — that network's own irregularity is *matched* by reclaimed material's irregularity, not fought by it. Three material families were researched specifically to test that fit: metal, GFRC, circular wood.

**2. Material Research (3 min)** — One line per family on source and structural behaviour; spend the most time on the honest sourcing gaps (GFRC's thin salvage stream, solved via basalt fibre substitution) since that's the more interesting story than the straightforward wins (metal roofing sheet, CLT offcuts).

**3. Material Catalogue (1.5 min)** — Same framing as before: research-stage, structured to receive real field data, each row a computational parameter set.

**4. Precedent Studies (4.5 min)** — Don't describe, go straight to lessons. Spend the most time on the Thin-Shell Nest Pavilion (main precedent) and the Sanchez/discrete-timber pairing, since together they represent the two competing computational strategies (growth vs. combinatorial) the pavilion concepts are testing against each other. Close on the cross-precedent synthesis table.

**5. Initial Pavilion Concepts (3.5 min)** — Present all four briefly. Be explicit that Concept D (Layered Hierarchy) is the safest/most resourced, while A and B most directly extend the strongest precedents — frame this trade-off honestly rather than picking a favourite before hearing feedback.

**6. Questions for Discussion**
- Given growth-algorithm (continuous, emergent) and Sanchez's combinatorial design (discrete, standardised) are genuinely different computational paradigms, should the pavilion commit to one, or is a hybrid (per Concept C) realistic within this semester's timeline?
- Is flexible-mould-table access realistic for the GFRC direction (Concept B), or should GFRC be scoped down to a secondary/accent role given that access constraint?
- With material sourcing not yet confirmed for any of the three families, how much should Wednesday's feedback push toward narrowing to one direction vs. keeping multiple options open into Week 4?
- Does the studio have a view on which of the two computational strategies (grade-then-place vs. standardise-the-joint) better demonstrates "reclaimed material as active generator of form" for assessment purposes?
