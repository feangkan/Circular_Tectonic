## The Brief (as given)

1. **Circular Material Research** — reclaimed/reusable construction materials: source, physical characteristics, dimensions, structural behaviour, advantages/limitations, environmental implications, existing applications; sketches/photos/diagrams/references.
2. **Material Catalogue** — digital catalogue: material type, dimensions, quantity, condition, fabrication/assembly methods, design opportunities — the foundation of the semester's computational workflow.
3. **Precedent Research** — 3–5 projects (reclaimed materials, circular construction, digital fabrication, modular assembly, computational design) with critical analysis: why the construction method was chosen, how materials shaped form, how structure/fabrication/assembly integrate, what lessons transfer.
4. **Initial Pavilion Exploration** — several early 3×3×3m pavilion ideas: design concept, material strategy, initial tectonic thinking, sketches/models/computational studies.

**Presentation:** 10–15 minutes, all four areas plus discussion questions.
