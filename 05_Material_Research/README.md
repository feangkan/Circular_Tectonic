# Material Research

Systematic exploration of material systems, fabrication methods, and performance characteristics.

## Subfolders

### GFRC/
Glass Fiber Reinforced Concrete research:
- Mix design documentation
- Test samples and results
- Surface finish exploration
- Aggregate experimentation
- Color/pigment studies

**Standard Mix (reference):**
- 25kg GRC Premix + 3875ml water + 50g iron oxide + 625g glass fibre

### Timber/
Timber component and hybrid system research:
- Species selection and properties
- Connection detailing
- Fabrication tolerances
- Durability & treatment
- Cost/availability analysis

### Circular-Materials/
Exploration of reclaimed, recycled, and bio-based alternatives:
- Material sourcing
- Performance data
- End-of-life pathways
- Cost comparison

### Testing/
Structural and material performance testing:
- Test methodology notes
- Load testing results
- Durability protocols
- Failure analysis
