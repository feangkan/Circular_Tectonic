## TIER 1: MOST RELEVANT — Study in Detail

### 1. Re-SPLAM Pavilion (Chicago, 2025)

**Architect:** Skidmore, Owings & Merrill (SOM)  
**Material:** Reclaimed timber (2×4 planks from demolished homes)  
**Status:** Currently installed, Chicago Architecture Biennial  
**Scale:** 16 × 20 feet

**Key Facts:**
<cite index="49-1">Optimized structural design delivers performance of reinforced concrete slabs at just one-fifth the weight, using exclusively reclaimed 2×4 planks with unique imperfections and varying structural integrity</cite>

**Innovation:**
- Material variability as design feature (imperfections are aesthetic)
- Computational optimization for non-uniform materials
- Circular approach to construction reuse

**Lessons for You:**
✓ Proof that reclaimed wood can be structurally optimized  
✓ Variability in material becomes design strategy  
✓ SOM's research on lightweight timber systems  
✓ Integration of parametric design with material constraints

**Link:** https://www.som.com/news/2025-biennial/

### 2. Hybrid Flax Pavilion (Germany, 2024)

**Architect:** University of Stuttgart (IntCDC Cluster)  
**Material:** Cross-laminated timber (CLT) + robotically wound flax fiber  
**Status:** Permanent exhibition building  
**Scale:** Medium pavilion (2024 Landesgartenschau)

**Key Innovation:**
<cite index="50-1">Combines cross-laminated timber (CLT) and robotically wound flax fiber to craft resource-efficient building made from regional and bio-based materials</cite>

**Material Strategy:**
- Primary: Thin CLT plates (reduced timber use vs. solid beams)
- Secondary: Flax fiber-reinforced composites for spanning elements
- Result: Reduced material use while maintaining performance

**Lessons for You:**
✓ Hybrid bio-based + wood systems possible  
✓ Robotic fabrication enabling precision in natural materials  
✓ Regional material sourcing (local flax production)  
✓ Mechanical connections for disassembly

**Link:** https://archello.com/news/innovative-hybrid-flax-pavilion-demonstrates-use-of-clt-and-flax-fiber-as-alternative-to-conventional-building-techniques

### 4. Re-Emerge Pavilion (London, 2022)

**Institution:** Architectural Association (EmTech) + Hassell Studio  
**Material:** Grade A reclaimed wood pallets  
**Scale:** Pavilion  
**Innovation:** Scoring + kerfing creates volumetric modules

**Key Facts:**
<cite index="54-1">Created with grade A reclaimed wood pallets; structural system comprises volumetric timber modules created by scoring and kerfing wood pallets, connected via lap joints creating diamond modules</cite>

**Material Processing:**
- Pallets (abundant waste material)
- Scoring + steam-bending (increases structural capacity)
- Lap joints (simple, traditional assembly)
- Kerfed geometry creates 3D structure from 2D sheets

**Lessons for You:**
✓ Upcycling pallets (most abundant reclaimed timber)  
✓ Computational design for material optimization  
✓ Simple assembly methods (lap joints, no fasteners)  
✓ Life Cycle Assessment integrated (shared with visitors via app)

**Link:** https://architizer.com/blog/practice/details/re-emerge-pavilion/

**Material:** Large-scale 3D-printed recycled polymer

**Innovation:** Biologically-inspired 3D printing

**Key Facts:**

**Fabrication:**

- Biological precedent-based design (flower geometry)
- Regional polymer partnerships
- Material efficiency through topology optimization

**Lessons for You:**
✓ 3D printing at architectural scale possible  
✓ Recycled polymer viability proven  
✓ Bio-inspired forms reduce material use  
✓ Material becomes structural logic

## DESIGN PRINCIPLES ACROSS PRECEDENTS

### Common Themes

1. **Material Reuse First**
   - Every precedent starts with available material
   - Design responds to what exists (material-driven)
   - Constraints become opportunities

2. **Hybrid Systems**
   - Rarely pure single-material solutions
   - Combine materials by function (structure vs. skin, durability vs. aesthetics)
   - Digital fabrication (3D printing) enables precision at material interfaces

3. **Visible Logic**
   - Connections are celebrated, not hidden
   - Material story told through assembly
   - Structural expression through material choice

4. **Disassembly Embedded**
   - Most designed for relocation/recycling at end-of-life
   - Bolted connections preferred over welded
   - Modular unit strategy enables this

5. **Local Sourcing**
   - Material sourcing specific to region/context
   - Reduces transportation embodied carbon
   - Creates unique local identity
   - Connects architecture to place

6. **Innovation Through Constraint**
   - Limited material palette forces creativity
   - Parametric design optimizes around limitations
   - Results in elegant, efficient structures
   - Better than unlimited budget
