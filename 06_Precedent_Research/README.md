# Precedent Research

Systematic study of relevant case studies, design practices, and architectural precedents.

## Subfolders

### Case-Studies/
Detailed analysis of built or research projects:
- Project documentation
- Design intent & strategy
- Material/tectonic approach
- Fabrication methods
- Outcomes & learnings

### Analogues/
Design and construction practices from adjacent fields:
- Industrial manufacturing processes
- Biological/natural systems
- Craft techniques
- Digital fabrication workflows

### Circular-Practices/
Organizations and methodologies in circular construction:
- Circular economy frameworks
- Design-for-disassembly systems
- Material passports & EPDs
- Deconstruction protocols
